# Notes — 05 sabbath-before-sinai

## Hook (on-screen, 0–1.5s)

> "The Sabbath existed before Sinai."

## Receipt (4–25s key beat)

Gen 2:3 — God sanctified the seventh day before there was a Jew.

## Source material

- [[genesis-1-2-sabbath-creation-foundation]] — `Torah/wiki/analyses/genesis-1-2-sabbath-creation-foundation.md`

`/articles/sabbath-creation-to-eternity`

## Key claims

- Gen 2:3 says God blessed and sanctified the seventh day at creation.
- This is centuries before Abraham, let alone Israel as a nation.
- The Sabbath is therefore a creation ordinance, not a Mosaic ceremonial law.

## Counter-arguments to expect

"Sabbath is just for Jews" → it was established in creation, before Israel existed.
