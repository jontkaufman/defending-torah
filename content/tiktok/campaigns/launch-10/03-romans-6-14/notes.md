# Notes — 03 romans-6-14

## Hook (on-screen, 0–1.5s)

> "Romans 6:14 doesn't say what you think."

## Receipt (4–25s key beat)

"Under law" = under penalty/condemnation, not under obligation.

## Source material

- [[romans-6-14-15-not-under-law-but-under-grace]] — `Torah/wiki/analyses/romans-6-14-15-not-under-law-but-under-grace.md`

## CTA target

`/articles/romans-6-14-not-under-law`

## Key claims

- The Greek `hypo nomon` ("under the law") means "under the law's condemnation/penalty".
- Paul immediately asks "shall we sin? God forbid" in v.15 — proving the Law still defines sin.
- Rom 3:19 uses the same phrase in a courtroom context.

## Counter-arguments to expect

"Paul says we're free from the law" → free from its condemnation, not its instruction.
