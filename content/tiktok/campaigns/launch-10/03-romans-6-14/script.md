## Hook (0–1.5s) — on-screen + spoken

Romans 6:14 doesn't say what you think.

## Pivot (1.5–4s)

Just read the next verse.

## Receipt (4–25s)

Verse 14: "For sin shall not be your master, because you are not under law but under grace."

The next sentence — verse 15: "What then? Shall we sin because we are not under law but under grace? By no means."

If "not under law" meant the Law is abolished, Paul's own question is incoherent. The Law is what defines sin in the first place.

## Twist (25–35s)

"Not under law" means not under its condemnation. Not under no obligation.
