## Receipt (4–25s key beat)

Read v.17-19 verbatim; Greek `pleroo` ≠ abolish.

## Key claims

- Jesus explicitly says he did not come to abolish the Law.
- `pleroo` means "fill up / complete the meaning of", not "terminate".
- v.19 ties consequences in the kingdom to obedience to even least commandments.

## Counter-arguments to expect

"Fulfill means complete/end" → that's `teleo`, not `pleroo`; receipt video 8.
