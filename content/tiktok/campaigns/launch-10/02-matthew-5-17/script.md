## Hook (0–1.5s) — on-screen + spoken

Pastors skip Matthew 5:17.

## Pivot (1.5–4s)

Jesus answers the abolish-the-Law question directly. Watch.

## Receipt (4–25s)

Verse 17: "Do not think that I came to abolish the Law or the Prophets. I did not come to abolish, but to fulfill."

The Greek for "abolish" is *kataluo* — destroy. The Greek for "fulfill" is *pleroo* — fill up, carry out completely.

Then verse 19: "Whoever sets aside one of the least of these commandments and teaches others to do the same will be called least in the kingdom of heaven."

## Twist (25–35s)

Jesus didn't end the Law. He warned against teaching others to.
