## Hook (0–1.5s) — on-screen + spoken

The early church kept Saturday.

## Pivot (1.5–4s)

And we know — because a preacher had to scream against it.

## Receipt (4–25s)

386 AD. Antioch. John Chrysostom delivers eight sermons titled *Adversus Judaeos* — "Against the Jews."

His complaint? Christians keep going to the synagogue on Saturday. They keep the Sabbath. They keep the feasts.

He had to preach against it because Christians were still doing it. Three hundred and fifty years after Jesus.

## Twist (25–35s)

Sunday didn't replace the Sabbath in scripture. It replaced it slowly — against resistance — by imperial pressure.
