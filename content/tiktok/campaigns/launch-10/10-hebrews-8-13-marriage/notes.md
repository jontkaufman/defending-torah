# Notes — 10 hebrews-8-13-marriage

## Hook (on-screen, 0–1.5s)

> "Hebrews 8:13 isn't about the Law."

## Receipt (4–25s key beat)

"Covenant" = marriage covenant being renewed, not Torah annulled.

## Source material

- [[hebrews-8-13-marriage-covenant]] — `Torah/wiki/analyses/hebrews-8-13-marriage-covenant.md`

## CTA target

`/articles/god-and-israel-the-marriage`

## Key claims

- Hebrews 8 quotes Jer 31, which is explicitly framed as God renewing his marriage covenant with Israel.
- The "old covenant" growing obsolete is the broken marriage arrangement, not the Torah (Jer 31:33 — the Law is *written on the heart* in the new covenant).
- Hebrews is contrasting covenant administrations, not Law vs. no-Law.

## Counter-arguments to expect

"The Old Covenant is obsolete" → covenant ≠ Torah; the covenant was the marriage to Israel, and Jer 31:33 puts the Law into the new covenant.
