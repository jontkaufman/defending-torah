## Receipt (4–25s key beat)

## Key claims

- Peter refused to eat the unclean animals three times.
- Acts 10:28 — Peter himself states "God has shown me that I should not call any man common or unclean".
- The vision was a teaching device about Gentile inclusion, not a dietary revocation.

## Counter-arguments to expect

"Peter ate the animals" → he didn't. He refused three times.
