## Hook (0–1.5s) — on-screen + spoken

Galatians isn't about the Law.

## Pivot (1.5–4s)

It's about one specific lie.

## Receipt (4–25s)

Acts 15. Men come from Judea teaching, quote: "Unless you are circumcised, you cannot be saved."

Galatians is Paul's response to that exact lie spreading into his churches.

Galatians 2 verse 3: "Titus, who was with me, though a Greek, was not compelled to be circumcised." That's the whole argument in one sentence.

## Twist (25–35s)

Paul fought circumcision-as-salvation. He never fought Torah. He kept Torah his whole life — Acts 21 proves it.
