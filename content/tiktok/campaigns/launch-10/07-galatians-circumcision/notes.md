## Key claims

- The occasion of Galatians is the Judaizer demand that Gentile converts be circumcised to be saved (Acts 15).
- Titus, a Greek, was deliberately NOT compelled to be circumcised — Paul's proof point.
- "Works of the law" in Galatians is shorthand for circumcision as a boundary marker, not Torah obedience in general.

## Counter-arguments to expect

"Paul calls the Law a curse" → he calls being under its penalty a curse (Gal 3:13), not the Law itself.
