# Notes — 04 mark-7-foods

## Hook (on-screen, 0–1.5s)

> "Mark 7 doesn't say all foods clean."

## Receipt (4–25s key beat)

KJV vs modern translation diff; the "declaring all foods clean" phrase is a translator's parenthetical insertion.

## Source material

- [[mark-7-declaring-all-foods-clean]] — `Torah/wiki/analyses/mark-7-declaring-all-foods-clean.md`

## CTA target

`/dietary-laws-what-scripture-says`

## Key claims

- The Greek does not contain "declaring all foods clean" as a direct statement of Jesus.
- The chapter is about hand-washing (rabbinic tradition), not Torah dietary law.
- Peter still considered unclean meat unclean in Acts 10 — would have known if Jesus had abolished it.

## Counter-arguments to expect

"But Jesus declared all foods clean" → that's a translator's parenthetical, not in the Greek.
