## Hook (0–1.5s) — on-screen + spoken

Mark 7 doesn't say all foods are clean.

## Pivot (1.5–4s)

Open your Bible. Look at the parenthesis.

## Receipt (4–25s)

Modern translations slip in: "Thus he declared all foods clean." That's a translator's parenthetical — not Jesus' words.

The chapter is about handwashing. Pharisees attacked Jesus for eating with unwashed hands. Jesus said *tradition* can't make food unclean — not Torah.

Pork wasn't on the table. Pork wasn't food.

## Twist (25–35s)

Ten years later, in Acts 10, Peter says: "I have never eaten anything unclean." If Jesus abolished kosher, Peter didn't get the memo.
